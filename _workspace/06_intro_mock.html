Ant's-Eye World

Shrink down. Walk the yard as a tiny ant.
Enter the Ant's-Eye World ▶

What will you do?

1. ExploreWalk from your ant nest across a big yard.
2. FindGo to the pink rings ✉. Read each ant problem.
3. DesignPick one problem. Draw and build something to fix it!

How to play

↑ W Walk: arrow keys or W A S D
🖱 Look around: drag the mouse
Enter Read a message when the pink button shows
🗺 The map (top right) shows you and the pink rings.
✔ A green check means you read that message.

What you need

💻 A laptop or Chromebook with the internet
⌨️ A keyboard and a mouse
🥽 Want to really feel like an ant?
Get a VR headset ready!
